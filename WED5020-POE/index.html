Brief introduction:
KE WAV Hub (W HUB)
is a South African youth streetwear brand
which has its roots in the underground and rebellious youth street culture that dominates the urban centers of Mzansi.
KE WAV HUB was founded in 2022 by two university roommates angry and frustrated by
the expensive fast fashion waste.

We are a streetwear brand that celebrates African culture and GenZ's style. Our unique designs are inspired by the vibrant energy of the streets, and we strive to create fashion that makes a statement. Join us in our mission to connect with our community and make a lasting impact.
