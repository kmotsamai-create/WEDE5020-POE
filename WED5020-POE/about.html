About KE WAV HUB

GEN Z Market HUB history's.

KE WAV Hub (W HUB) is a South African youth streetwear brand
which has its roots in the underground and rebellious youth street culture that dominates the urban centers of Mzansi.
GenZ was founded in 2022 by two uni roommates angry and frustrated by the expensive fast fashion waste.

KE WAV HUB mission and vision.

KE WAV HUB is to empower youth culture through authentic,
eco-conscious streetwear while actively dismantling the fast-fashion waste cycle.
We provide accessible, high-quality, and upcycled fashion
that lets Gen Z express their identity without compromising the planet.
Our vision as GEN Z Market HUB is to lead a global retail revolution where circular fashion is the industry default,
creating a world where every garment tells a story,
lasts a lifetime, and
leaves zero footprint.
